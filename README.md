A [make.fun](https://make.fun) bounty entry: a monologue of exactly 100 words,
spoken by a fantasy villain who is secretly just very tired.

## The monologue

Malachar, lord of the Ashen Spire, has waited three hundred years for the
eclipse that will blacken the moonwell and break the seals. His brother's
ghost still owes him an apology, his ravens have left, and his ledgers have
stayed. When the hero climbs the steps, sword in hand, Malachar does not
roar. He asks the hero to put the sword down a moment, offers the small
chair instead of the throne, forgets what he was saying mid-sentence, and
nearly gives the kingdom away for a blanket and cold tea — until, in the
last line, he remembers the ritual.

His tiredness is never announced. It lives in the pauses, the small
deflections, the stairs, and the chair that is not the throne.

## How it runs

`main.py` (pure Python standard library, no dependencies) writes the
monologue to `/output/entry.txt`. It is fully deterministic: the same
100 words, byte for byte, on every run. The program self-checks the word
count (exactly 100) and the gate (1–600 characters) before writing.

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
